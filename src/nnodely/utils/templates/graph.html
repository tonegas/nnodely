How to read this graph

The graph is the computation of the model. Each card is a node: its name on top, what it is below. Arrows carry values from one node to the next, and are labelled with the shape of what they carry.

Nodes

Arrows

Shapes

A shape is written (seq…, tw, dim…), the batch left out. seq are sequence axes - the steps of a rollout, for instance - and dyn marks a length set by the data; tw is the window, how many samples are read at once; dim are the features of each sample.

Views

  • Standard: every sub-model - a model called inside another, or a Loop - is one node. Double-click it to open its body on a page of its own.
  • Flattened: every sub-model is spliced in, its body inside a shaded region, and the recurrence of a Loop drawn as a feedback arrow.

Exploring

  • Click a node to see what it is, its shape and its connections; click an arrow for what it carries.
  • From the panel, highlight everything a node depends on or feeds.
  • Click an entry of the legend to highlight every node of that kind.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan, drag a node to move it.
